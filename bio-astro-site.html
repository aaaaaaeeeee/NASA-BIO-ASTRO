The solution

Meet BIO-ASTRO.

A wristband the astronaut wears at all times that tracks their vital signs nonstop.

Its AI runs on board, not on Earth: built on open NASA data (OSDR, Human Research Program), it compares every reading with the astronaut’s own baseline and spots risk before it becomes an emergency.

Back to Said

From signal to action, in five steps.

1

Detects

His heart rate and oxygen leave his personal range.

2

Classifies

The AI checks his history and NASA data: possible hypoxia.

3

Alerts

The wristband vibrates and warns the crew, showing where he is on the ship.

4

Guides

A step-by-step protocol: what to check, what to do, what to measure again.

5

Records

A medical log that travels to Earth as soon as there’s a link.

Forty minutes become seconds.

Why us

Not just another pulse watch.

It’s a system that, with no link to Earth, turns a signal into coordinated action by the whole crew. And we already have a working prototype of the platform:
